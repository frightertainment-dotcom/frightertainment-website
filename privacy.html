SITE INFORMATION

PRIVACY & COOKIES.

Last updated: 11 October 2026. This notice explains how Frightertainment handles information on the website.

Who to contact

Frightertainment operates this independent horror entertainment website. For privacy enquiries or requests about information you have sent us, contact Frightertainment@gmail.com. You can browse without opening an account.

Information collected

If you write to us, we receive your name, reply email address, subject and message. Please do not send sensitive personal information. Cloudflare may process technical and security information when a visitor accesses the site. Your selected cinema territory may be kept in browser storage to remember your preference.

External web-font servers, poster providers and embedded media services may receive browser connection information when their resources load.

Purpose and legal basis

We use correspondence to answer enquiries, consider recommendations and respond to relevant follow-up. Our usual lawful basis is legitimate interests in responding to messages and running an independent website. Site-security data supports the legitimate interest in detecting abuse and protecting the service. Any other specific purpose and lawful basis must be considered separately.

Service providers and external content

Cloudflare provides site hosting, security and performance services. The Frightertainment email inbox is hosted by Google. Google Fonts can deliver fonts, while artwork may come from TMDB and other credited providers. Apple Podcasts, Steam and YouTube may receive information when their embedded players or linked pages are loaded. These services have their own privacy practices.

Service providers may process data outside the United Kingdom. Contact us for information about relevant international data-transfer safeguards.

Cookies and local preferences

The protected owner-only preview uses Cloudflare Access authentication cookies. The site uses browser local storage for your selected release territory; you can remove this information through your browser settings. Cloudflare real-user performance monitoring may run for eligible traffic; Cloudflare states its beacon does not read or store cookies or local storage.

The current Frightertainment code does not intentionally place targeted-advertising cookies. Embedded Apple Podcasts and Steam players are disabled until you choose to load each player. Choosing LOAD may connect your browser to Apple or Steam, which can use their own cookies or similar technologies. If you do not choose to load the player, it will not contact that provider through the embedded player. You can continue browsing Frightertainment without loading either player.

Retention

Enquiries are retained as long as necessary to reply, handle reasonable follow-up and satisfy any applicable legal obligations. The relevant period depends on the nature of each enquiry. You may request deletion of information that is no longer needed, subject to legal exceptions.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable rules, you may ask to access, correct, erase or restrict personal data, request portability where relevant, or object to its use. You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact Frightertainment@gmail.com to make a request.

You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
