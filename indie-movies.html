About these charts

Indie Horror is a source-checked selection of independently developed films, including festival and filmmaker discoveries. Mainstream studio franchises are excluded. A film distributed by a larger company may still be independently produced.

Chart positions use TMDB viewer ratings with at least 50 votes, only for released films in the source-checked collection. Some indie films have no available rating or official trailer; festival credits and original film year remain accessible.
