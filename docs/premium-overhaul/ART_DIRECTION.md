## Anti-template review

For each pass, inspect 320 and 1440 first/full page, then 360/390/430/768/1024/1920 and long titles. Ask: could the section be swapped with another after changing the headline? Are there repeated same-size panels, borders or giant empty gradients? Does real media or truthful metadata carry the composition? Is the first action useful and is the next route equally finished? Keep brand character through imagery, rhythm and typography rather than fake film posters or decorative gimmicks.
